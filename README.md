## You found a workflow. What happens next?

Public workflows are useful because they make the idea tangible. But the real value is making the system fit your people, rules, tools, exceptions, and customers.

OptiFlix helps you move from **“this could help us”** to a workflow your team can actually trust:

1. **Start with the work, not the hype.** We map what happens today, where information gets lost, and what the process is costing in time, delays, and rework.
2. **Choose the right level of help.** Use a ready-made workflow, get an expert second opinion, or have us build the complete system around your operation.
3. **Leave with something usable.** We work toward clear scope, practical integrations, sensible error handling, documentation, and ownership you can understand.

## About the public workflows

The workflows in our public repositories are shared to help people learn, test ideas, and get moving. Before using one in production, review its credentials, permissions, input assumptions, error paths, privacy requirements, and destination systems.

If you want, send us the workflow you found and tell us:

- what your business does;
- which step you want to improve;
- what tools you already use; and
- what a successful result would look like.

We can tell you whether the public workflow is a good fit, what needs changing, or whether a simpler approach would be better.
